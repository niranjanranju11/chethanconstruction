# 10 — SEO Specification

## 1. Core
Every public page needs:
- Unique title.
- Meta description.
- Canonical URL.
- Open Graph title/description/image.
- Twitter/X card metadata where useful.

## 2. Target intent
Do not keyword-stuff. Optimize naturally for the actual service area and services.

Potential phrases after confirming location:
- construction company in [city]
- residential construction in [city]
- house construction contractor in [city]
- commercial construction in [city]
- villa construction in [city]

Never claim a service/location the company does not provide.

## 3. Project SEO
Each published project:
- Unique slug.
- Unique title.
- Useful description.
- Location if appropriate.
- Relevant image.
- Canonical URL.

## 4. Structured data
Consider:
- Organization/LocalBusiness schema.
- Service schema.
- BreadcrumbList.
- ImageObject.
- Project/CreativeWork-style structured data where appropriate.

Only include facts actually present on the site.

## 5. Sitemap
Generate `/sitemap.xml` containing:
- Home.
- About.
- Services.
- Published project pages.
- Contact.

Do not include admin pages.

## 6. robots.txt
Allow public content.
Disallow:
- `/admin`
- private API routes
- internal paths as appropriate.

## 7. Images
Use:
- Descriptive filenames where practical.
- Accurate alt text.
- Responsive sizes.
- Modern formats.

Avoid keyword-stuffed alt text.

## 8. Performance
Target strong Core Web Vitals:
- Optimize hero image.
- Lazy load galleries.
- Avoid huge JavaScript bundles.
- Compress images.
- Preload only critical assets.

## 9. Local presence
If the company has a legitimate physical/service location, align website business information with its verified business listing and keep name/phone/address consistent.

## 10. Analytics
Optional:
- Privacy-conscious analytics.
- Search Console.

Do not add invasive tracking without business/privacy requirements.
